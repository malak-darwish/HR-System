Job Title: Registered Nurse – Intensive Care Unit (ICU)

Location: Beirut, Lebanon
Employment Type: Full-time

About the Role:
We are seeking a compassionate and skilled Registered Nurse to join our Intensive Care Unit. The nurse will provide direct care to critically ill patients, monitor vital signs, administer medications, and work closely with physicians and the multidisciplinary care team.

Responsibilities:
- Assess, plan, and deliver nursing care to critically ill patients
- Monitor patients on ventilators, cardiac monitors, and infusion pumps
- Administer medications and IV therapies according to physician orders
- Respond to medical emergencies and assist in resuscitation procedures
- Maintain accurate patient records and nursing documentation
- Educate patients and families on care plans and discharge instructions

Mandatory Requirements:
- Bachelor of Science in Nursing (BSN)
- Valid Registered Nurse license from the Order of Nurses in Lebanon
- Minimum 3 years of clinical experience in an ICU or critical care setting
- Current Basic Life Support (BLS) and Advanced Cardiovascular Life Support (ACLS) certifications
- Strong knowledge of critical care procedures, patient monitoring, and medication administration
- Ability to work rotating shifts, including nights, weekends, and holidays

Preferred Qualifications:
- Experience with electronic health record (EHR) systems
- Fluency in Arabic, English, and French
